Getting started with Lumencia is simple. Here’s how it works.

1. Fill in the enquiry form

Start on our Home page and complete the short enquiry form. Tell us about your business, what you offer, and how people usually contact you.

2. We’ll be in touch and agree your plan

We contact you to discuss your needs, recommend a plan, and confirm the monthly price. You stay in control and can ask any questions.

3. Sign a simple contract

We send a clear contract so our work is protected while you keep full ownership of your content and intellectual property. There are no hidden extras.

4. Set up secure monthly payment

We send a secure payment request for your chosen plan. Payments are monthly and you’re not locked into a long-term agreement – you can cancel month by month.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the monthly subscription?

Your subscription covers full website hosting, domain management, regular security updates, and ongoing content changes. We handle all the technical details so you can focus on running your business.

Are there any hidden setup fees?

No, Lumencia is built on a transparent monthly subscription model. We do not charge upfront design or development fees, making a professional web presence accessible for local businesses.

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?

Yes, our service is designed for flexibility. You are not locked into a long-term contract and can cancel your plan whenever you choose without penalty.

How do you handle customer enquiries?

We integrate streamlined enquiry systems directly into your site. These are designed to capture leads efficiently and ensure you get notified immediately so no potential customer is missed.

Do I need to be tech-savvy?

Not at all. Lumencia is a managed service. You tell us what you need, and we take care of the rest. We specifically cater to non-technical business owners who need a professional digital presence without the hassle.
